04 / The fine print

Clear by default.

Commitments written down, without the small print pretending to be big.

01 A personal, non-commercial initiative +

NggKhaiz Studio is a personal open-source effort, not a company and not a registered organisation. Nothing here is monetised: no advertising, no paid tiers, no data sold.

Projects are published for learning, for use, and for review. Maintenance and support follow the free time of one person, so there is no uptime promise attached.

02 Zero data collection +

These pages ship no analytics, no advertising trackers and no telemetry. No cookies are set for tracking, and the fonts are served from this domain.

Two preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage only: the interface theme and the language. Clearing site data removes both. Links to GitHub take you to a service outside this site, under its own policies.

03 Third-party content and services +

Projects are independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representatives of any third-party platform or service unless stated explicitly.

Third-party services may change or disappear at any time. Software is provided as available, without guarantee of uninterrupted service.

04 Source code licensing +

Source is published in public repositories for transparent review. Permission to use, modify and redistribute each project is governed only by the license included in that repository.

Check the LICENSE file before redistributing. Third-party dependencies keep their own licenses and attribution requirements. The typefaces here are Inter, Inter Tight and JetBrains Mono, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
