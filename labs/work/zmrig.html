What it is

A RandomX miner with a developer fee you can see and switch off, the pool that pays for it, a proxy for the whole farm, and the site that puts it together.

How it ships

Source and release builds. GPL-3.0, with the fee documented in doc/FEE.md instead of buried in code.

What is next

Small fixes, clearer docs and whatever the next idea needs. There is no roadmap beyond that, and no promise of one.
