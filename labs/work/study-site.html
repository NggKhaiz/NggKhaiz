Study Focus Fox

Study Focus Fox — one HTML file to study Physics 9 and everything else.

A study tool, offline · 2026

What it is

A single-file study environment: a 52-session Physics 9 track, spaced repetition, grade book, weekly plans and a pixel arena. Offline by default, because studying should not need an account.

How it ships

One HTML file. Open it, no install, no login, nothing leaves the device.

What is next

Small fixes, clearer docs and whatever the next idea needs. There is no roadmap beyond that, and no promise of one.
